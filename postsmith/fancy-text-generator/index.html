How to make fancy text for your bio

  1. Type your text. Enter a name, bio line or caption in the box. Every style updates as you type.
  2. Pick a style. Filter by group (bold, script, bubbles, lines, flipped) or turn on “Only screen-reader-friendly”.
  3. Copy it. Click Copy next to a style and paste it into Instagram, TikTok, X, Discord or anywhere that accepts text.
  4. Pin your favorites. Click the star to keep a style at the top next time. It’s saved only in your browser.

Fancy Text Generator: frequently asked questions

Are these real fonts?

No. They’re Unicode characters from the math, enclosed and phonetic blocks that look like styled letters. Because they’re characters, not formatting, they survive copy and paste into apps that don’t support bold or italic.

Do fancy fonts hurt accessibility?

Often, yes. Screen readers may read 𝐇𝐞𝐥𝐥𝐨 as “mathematical bold capital H…”, or skip it entirely. Each style here has a badge: “Reads OK” styles (like full-width or combining lines) are the safest, “Reads poorly” and “Unreadable” ones should be limited to a word or two.

Will fancy text show up in search?

Usually not. Platform search and Google treat 𝓃𝒶𝓂𝑒 and name as different words. Keep your real name and keywords in plain text so people can find you.

Why do some letters look plain or missing?

Unicode doesn’t have every letter in every style (for example small caps has no Q or X, and superscript lacks some letters), so we fall back to the closest available character. Older devices may also show boxes for rare symbols.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or logged.
