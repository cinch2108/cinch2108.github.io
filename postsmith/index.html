Questions, answered

Is Postsmith free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no account, credits or watermark. The site is supported by optional, clearly marked ads.

Is what I type sent to a server?

No. All six tools run entirely in your browser. Your text, images and link-in-bio projects never leave your device, and the site has no analytics or tracking scripts.

Are the character limits up to date?

They were checked in October 2026, and the counter follows each platform’s real counting rules, including X’s weighted emoji and links. Platforms change limits occasionally, so the date is shown.

Are the hashtags trending?

No, and we won’t pretend otherwise. The generator works offline from curated topic lists and your keywords, which gives relevant tags but no live trend data.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in any modern browser on phone or desktop. You can add it to your home screen, and it keeps working offline after the first visit.

Can I use the link-in-bio page commercially?

Yes. The exported HTML file is yours to host and use however you like, with no attribution required. You can turn off the small “Made with Postsmith” credit.
