How to get the right image size

  1. Find your placement. Search for “story”, “banner” or a number, or filter by platform.
  2. Check the safe zone. The preview shows the area that apps cover with buttons or crop away.
  3. Download a template. Choose a background (white, transparent, black or custom), toggle guides and labels, then download one PNG or a ZIP for the whole platform.
  4. Design and export. Open the template in your editor (Canva, Figma, Photoshop) and design inside the guides.

Image Size Guide: frequently asked questions

What size is an Instagram post in 2026?

1080 × 1350 px (4:5) for portrait, which takes the most screen space. Instagram’s profile grid now shows 3:4 thumbnails, so 1080 × 1440 avoids grid cropping. Square posts are 1080 × 1080 and Stories/Reels are 1080 × 1920.

What is the best size for a YouTube thumbnail?

1280 × 720 px (16:9), under 2 MB, as JPG or PNG. Keep important text away from the bottom-right corner, where the video length appears.

What is a safe zone?

The part of the image that stays visible and uncovered. On Stories, Reels and TikTok, the profile name, caption and buttons cover the top and bottom, so keep text and faces inside the guides.

Are the templates free to use?

Yes. They’re plain PNGs drawn in your browser, with no watermark and no sign-up. Use them for anything.

How current are these sizes?

They were reviewed in October 2026. Platforms change specs without notice, so double-check anything critical in the platform’s own help center.
