Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-25. This is template text. Have it reviewed before launch.

What you type and upload

Every tool on Postsmith runs entirely inside your browser. Text you write, images you add to the link-in-bio builder and the files you export are processed on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. The site’s Content Security Policy only allows network requests back to this site (to load the app itself).

What is stored on your device

  • Preferences: theme, favorite and recent tools (ps-theme, ps-favs, ps-recent).
  • Drafts and tool settings, so your work is still there after a reload: ps-fx-*, ps-ct-*, ps-ht, ps-em-*, ps-sz and ps-bio (your link-in-bio project, including the photo you chose).
  • Your consent choice (ps-consent).
  • Offline cache: a service worker stores the site’s own code so it works offline.

All of this stays in your browser’s localStorage and cache. Clear your site data at any time to remove it.

Advertising

The site may show ads to stay free. No ad code loads unless you choose “Allow ads” in the consent banner, and you can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer. Ad partners would be listed here once configured.

Analytics

None are installed.

Exported link-in-bio pages

Pages you export contain no scripts and make no external requests. If you add analytics to your own hosted page, that’s between you and your visitors.
