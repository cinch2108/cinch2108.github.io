How to generate hashtags for a post

  1. Describe your post. Type a few keywords or paste your caption, such as “sourdough bread recipe for beginners”.
  2. Choose your platform. We auto-select the recommended number: 3–5 on Instagram and TikTok, 1–2 on X, 1 on Threads, and so on.
  3. Adjust the mix. Tap tags to add or remove them, add a location, shuffle for variety, or switch on #CamelCase.
  4. Copy and post. Copy the selected set in one click.

Hashtag Generator: frequently asked questions

How many hashtags should I use?

Fewer than you think. Instagram now caps posts at 5 hashtags, TikTok recommends 3–5, X works best with 1–2, Threads allows a single topic tag, LinkedIn suggests 3–5, and YouTube ignores all hashtags if you add more than 15.

Are these trending hashtags?

No. This tool runs offline from hand-curated topic lists plus your own keywords. It doesn’t see live trends, volumes or banned-tag lists. Search a tag on the platform before you rely on it.

Should I use broad or niche hashtags?

Mostly niche. Broad tags like #food have huge competition, so posts disappear in seconds. Niche and specific tags like #sourdoughbaking reach people who actually care. The auto-pick uses one broad tag and fills the rest with specific ones.

What is #CamelCase and why use it?

Capitalizing each word (#SmallBusinessOwner) makes tags easier to read and lets screen readers pronounce them as words. Mastodon users especially expect it.

Are #fyp and #viral useful?

Not really. They’re so generic they don’t tell the algorithm who your post is for. The tool flags them if you add them.
