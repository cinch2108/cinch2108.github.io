Edit PDF metadata: frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly, and your PDF never leaves your device. The site has no upload endpoint, and its Content Security Policy blocks sending file data anywhere.

Why does PDF metadata matter?

The title appears in browser tabs and search results, and the author and app fields can reveal personal or company information you may not want to share.

What does “Remove all metadata” remove?

It deletes the document information dictionary (title, author, dates, creator app and so on) and the XMP metadata stream.

Does it change the content of the pages?

No. Only the document information is changed.

Can I edit keywords for SEO?

Yes. Enter comma-separated keywords. Some search engines and document systems read them.

Is it really free? Are there limits?

Yes, it’s free with no sign-up and no watermark. There are no daily task limits or file-size caps; the only limit is your device’s memory.
