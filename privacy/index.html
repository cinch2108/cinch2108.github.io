Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-25. Placeholder text. Have it reviewed before launch.

Your files

Every PDF and image you open is processed entirely inside your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Files are never uploaded, stored on a server, or seen by us. The site’s Content Security Policy only allows network connections back to this site (to load the app itself), never to third parties.

What is stored on your device

  • Theme, favorites and recently used tools in localStorage (keys lf-theme, lf-favs, lf-recent).
  • Your consent choice in localStorage (lf-consent).
  • “Continue with” hand-off: when you click a “Continue with” button, the result file is kept in this browser’s IndexedDB for up to 30 minutes so the next tool can open it. It’s deleted when used, dismissed, or expired.
  • Offline cache: a service worker caches the app’s code (never your files) so it works offline.

Advertising

The site is funded by ads. No ad code loads unless you choose “Allow ads” in the consent banner. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer. Ad partners would be listed here once configured.

Analytics

None are installed.
